ABOUT THE PROGRAMME

Shaping understanding.
Inspiring leadership.

Learn about Seychelles National Model United Nations and our commitment to youth diplomacy.

OUR PURPOSE

National programme, international outlook

Established as a programme of the Tyler Nicholas Foundation, Seychelles National Model United Nations provides a national platform for young people to explore diplomacy, international relations and multilateral cooperation.

Our educational approach

Through realistic United Nations simulations, participants research global issues, represent assigned Member State positions, develop arguments, negotiate with others and practice respectful debate.

Core principles

The programme is grounded in diplomacy, academic independence, non-partisanship, cooperation, research, public speaking and respect for different perspectives.

Institutional relationship

SNMUN operates under the Tyler Nicholas Foundation. It is a Foundation programme, not a separate foundation or association.

OUR ORIGINS

Why SNMUN Was Established

Seychelles National Model United Nations was conceived as an educational response to the importance of international understanding, informed youth participation and opportunities to experience diplomacy beyond the classroom.

The programme was established under the Tyler Nicholas Foundation to provide a dedicated national framework for Model United Nations learning, diplomatic dialogue and leadership development.

Its founding approach brings together academic simulations, training, school participation and year-round activities within one coherent programme identity.

NATIONAL IDENTITY

A Programme Rooted in Seychelles

The programme’s national character reflects the place of Seychelles in the international community and the importance of equipping young people with the confidence to understand and discuss global challenges.

SNMUN is designed to create pathways for eligible young people from educational institutions and communities across Seychelles to take part in a shared diplomatic learning experience.

Its development is guided by an institutional commitment to non-partisanship, academic independence, dialogue and respect.

THE NEXT CHAPTER

Looking Ahead

SNMUN is in its programme development and establishment phase. Its proposed activities and future sessions will be communicated through official announcements when arrangements are confirmed.

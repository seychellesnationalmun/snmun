Introducing SNMUN

A national vision.
A global perspective.

Seychelles National Model United Nations is a national youth diplomacy, international relations and leadership education programme established under the Tyler Nicholas Foundation.

By bringing young people together through realistic United Nations simulations, research, public speaking and constructive dialogue, SNMUN strengthens understanding of the world and the responsibilities of international cooperation.
